# Adoption evidence and route to competitive strength

Observed 2026-10-29. Activity measures are not interchangeable with active humans, paying customers, retention or satisfaction.

| Project | Retrieved evidence | Honest interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Bittensor | Public explorer reports 6,254 distinct signing accounts for full UTC day 2026-10-28 and 128 subnets; daily counting methodology stated | Observable chain activity, including automated actors; not verified active human customers. Explorer measurement, not an independently reproduced chain audit here |
| Radicle | Project FAQ reports almost 8,000 repositories and over 600 weekly online nodes observed by its public seed nodes as of April 2026 | Meaningful observed network footprint, partial and older than today; nodes/repos are not unique active users |
| Drips | Project reports almost 600 participants and >3,000 merged PRs across 200+ repositories in the first Stellar Wave (February 2026 retrospective) | Evidence of a funded contribution event; not current monthly retention. Current program pages also exist, but cumulative/event counts should not be relabeled DAU/MAU |
| Gensyn | Current testnet documentation says RL Swarm and Gensyn-hosted nodes paused, BlockAssist/CodeAssist sunset, focus on Delphi | Historical participation cannot be counted as active present use of those services. No verified comparable current human-user total obtained |
| SingularityNET | Current service marketplace and payment documentation verified | Offering exists; trustworthy current DAU/MAU/retention figures not verified in this review |
| URACE | Owner trial, isolated engineering tests and incomplete matched cohorts | No independently verified external active-user base or market fit established |

URACE has a credible product direction; a great competitive position is not established. Feature breadth, code size, tests, token holders and synthetic engineering results cannot substitute for retained user benefit.

The strongest next validation sequence is: stabilize source-bound local improvement -> complete eligible matched comparisons -> independent pilot products -> measure continued use, accepted useful outcomes, regressions/interventions and complete cost -> validate that peer sharing adds net benefit -> enable paid settlement only if demonstrated demand and security/economic gates justify it. Integration with existing networks may be more efficient than replicating their infrastructure.

Use opt-in, privacy-preserving pilot reporting with clear denominators and periods. Count distinct consenting organizations/products and repeated useful deliveries; distinguish installation, activation, trial, retained use and paid use. Keep unknowns unknown; do not infer people from wallets, machines or repository IDs. No hidden telemetry, outreach or public launch has been authorized by preparing this document.

Sources:

* https://radicle.dev/faq — project-reported April 2026 footprint.

* https://docs.gensyn.ai/testnet — current phase and paused/sunset applications.
* https://dev.singularitynet.io/docs/products/AIMarketplace/ — documented marketplace scope, not adoption counts.

Research is a point-in-time snapshot. Network/project status can change; verify again before using it in external positioning or making deployment decisions. No numerical probability of success or market leadership is justified by these observations.
